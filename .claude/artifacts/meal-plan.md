## ⚠️ Read First — Your Two Health Flags

### 1. Black Coffee + GERD (you asked to include it)
Caffeine relaxes the lower esophageal sphincter → **worsens reflux**. To keep it without flaring up:
- **Never on an empty stomach.** Drink *after* food, not as your first item at 11 AM.
- **Max 1 cup/day**, before ~4 PM (caffeine late = poor sleep on a 2 AM bedtime).
- If you get burning/regurgitation within ~1 h, switch to a low-acid cold brew or drop it.
- It's in the plan at the **post-breakfast** slot so there's food in your stomach.

### 2. B12 Deficiency
You're veg + eggs, so dietary B12 comes mainly from **eggs, dairy (curd/paneer/milk), and fortified foods** — all featured here.
- **Diet alone usually can't correct an existing deficiency.** Keep taking your prescribed B12 supplement/shots until your doctor confirms levels are normal.
- This plan maximizes B12-rich foods daily (eggs + curd + milk/paneer) to *maintain* once corrected.

### GERD ground rules applied throughout
- Minimal tomato, citrus, raw onion, deep-fried, chocolate, mint, excess chili.
- Smaller, more frequent meals; chew slowly.
- Tomato/lemon are listed as **optional/garnish** — skip if symptomatic.

## 📅 DAY 1 — MONDAY
**Daily total: ~1,600 cal | P: ~148 g | C: ~140 g | F: ~50 g**

### 🌅 BREAKFAST — 11:30 AM · Moong Dal Chilla + Curd + Egg
**Servings: 1 | Time: 20 min | 420 cal | P: 34g | C: 40g | F: 14g**

INGREDIENTS:
- Yellow moong dal: 1/2 cup (soaked 2–3 h, ground) *or* besan 1/2 cup for speed
- Grated carrot + spinach: 1/2 cup
- Eggs: 1 whole + 2 whites (boiled or scrambled, on the side)
- Ginger (grated), cumin, hing, salt | Oil: 2 tsp (for 2 chillas)
- Low-fat / hung curd: 3/4 cup

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Blend soaked moong dal with ginger + a little water to a pancake batter.
2. Mix in grated veg, cumin, hing, salt.
3. Heat non-stick tava, ladle batter, spread thin; drizzle 1 tsp oil; cook 2–3 min/side till golden. Make 2.
4. Boil/scramble 1 egg + 2 whites (no chili).
5. Serve chillas with curd + the egg.

QUICK TIPS: The 2 extra whites + the bigger curd portion are what push this to 34 g protein. Besan chilla needs no soaking.
BUDGET: ~₹50/serving.

☕ **Black coffee (post-breakfast, ~12:15 PM):** 1 small cup, no sugar — *with food already in stomach.* (~5 cal)

### ☀️ LUNCH — 2:30 PM · No-Tomato Egg Curry + Rice
**Servings: 1 | Time: 25 min | 450 cal | P: 34g | C: 50g | F: 16g**

INGREDIENTS:
- Eggs: 3 (boiled) + 2 extra whites
- Curd: 1/3 cup (base instead of tomato — GERD-safe)
- Ginger, cumin, coriander powder, turmeric, hing, salt | Oil: 1.5 tsp
- Cooked rice: 3/4 cup

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Heat oil, add cumin, hing, ginger.
2. Add turmeric + coriander powder; stir 30 sec on low.
3. Whisk curd, lower heat, add slowly stirring (prevents curdling); add 1/2 cup water.
4. Add halved boiled eggs + chopped whites; simmer 5 min, salt.
5. Serve over rice.

QUICK TIPS: Curd gravy = creamy without acidic tomato. The extra whites lift protein with almost no fat. Keep flame low when adding curd.
BUDGET: ~₹45/serving.

### 🍵 EVENING SNACK — 6:30 PM · Sprouts & Tofu Salad
**Servings: 1 | Time: 8 min | 190 cal | P: 18g | C: 16g | F: 7g**

INGREDIENTS:
- Steamed moong sprouts: 3/4 cup
- Tofu cubes: 90 g (leaner than paneer)
- Cucumber + carrot, diced: 1/2 cup
- Coriander, roasted cumin, salt, pepper

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Steam sprouts 3–4 min (easier to digest than raw → better for gas/GERD).
2. Lightly pan-toast tofu cubes dry (2 min) or use raw.
3. Toss with diced veg + seasoning.

QUICK TIPS: Steaming > raw for your gastritis. Tofu keeps fat lower than paneer at this protein.
BUDGET: ~₹35/serving.

### 🥛 WHEY SHAKE — 7:30 PM · 1 Scoop
**1 scoop fermented whey + 250 ml water | ~130 cal | P: 25g | C: 4g | F: 2g** (water-based; this is your only whey serving for the day.)

### 🌙 DINNER — 9:30 PM · Palak Tofu + 1 Roti
**Servings: 1 | Time: 20 min | 410 cal | P: 37g | C: 30g | F: 11g**

INGREDIENTS:
- Spinach: 2 cups (blanched + puréed)
- Tofu: 200 g, cubed (firm; leaner than paneer)
- Egg whites: 2 (optional, stirred in for extra protein)
- Ginger, cumin, hing, coriander powder, salt | Oil: 1 tsp
- Whole wheat roti: 1

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Blanch spinach 2 min; blend smooth.
2. Heat oil, add cumin, hing, ginger.
3. Add spinach purée + coriander powder + salt; simmer 5 min.
4. Add tofu cubes; simmer 3 min. (Stir in egg whites in the last minute if using.)
5. Serve with 1 roti. (No cream/tomato — clean & GERD-safe.)

QUICK TIPS: 200 g tofu carries the protein here for ~14 g fat — paneer would nearly double the fat. Press tofu first for texture.
BUDGET: ~₹55/serving.

## 💡 GERD + Gastritis Daily Habits

1. **Don't skip to one big meal** despite the late start — 4–5 small meals beat 2 large ones for reflux.
2. **Sit upright 2–3 h after dinner.** Last bite by ~11 PM (you sleep ~2 AM). No late-night snacking.
3. **Elevate head of bed** ~15 cm if you get night reflux.
4. **Hydrate between meals**, not large volumes during meals.
5. **Hing (asafoetida) + ginger** in cooking → less bloating/gas (matters with the rajma/chana days).
6. **Track your triggers** — coffee, large portions, legumes, eggs in volume, and any tomato/citrus you do eat. Adjust.
7. **Egg volume:** you're eating more eggs now to hit protein on 1 scoop whey. If that ever feels heavy on reflux, lean harder on tofu and hung curd (both included) instead.
